Meet Rebecca

Specialist paediatric physiotherapist with more than 15 years of NHS and clinical experience — coming to your home across South London.

Background & experience

Rebecca qualified as a Physiotherapist in 2008 from the University of Birmingham, bringing more than 15 years of clinical experience to every session — 12 of those within the NHS — working with babies, children and teenagers across hospitals, homes, nurseries and schools.

She previously worked at the Royal Manchester Children's Hospital, where she gained extensive experience in paediatric neurology, respiratory care and orthopaedics. Her true passion, however, lies in musculoskeletal physiotherapy, which she chose to specialise in at Kingston Hospital in 2017.

This breadth of experience gives Rebecca a genuinely holistic and comprehensive approach — combining evidence-based clinical practice with real warmth and patience for every child and family she works with.

My approach

I believe that effective paediatric physiotherapy must be as engaging as it is functional. My focus is on creating fun, interactive, evidence-based programmes that children actually enjoy — while ensuring that home exercises are realistic and sustainable for busy families to integrate into their daily routines.

I work with children of all ages across a wide range of conditions, always making sure that parents feel informed, involved and confident in every step of the plan.

"As a mum myself, I live the reality of a busy family schedule — and I build that understanding into everything I do."

Why home visits?

All sessions are delivered as home visits — I come to your family, rather than the other way around. Children are assessed and treated in the environment they know best, with their own toys, equipment and familiar surroundings to hand.

For families with younger children, or children who find new settings challenging, this can make a significant difference to how relaxed and cooperative they feel — and how effective the session is.

It also simply makes life easier for busy parents. No travelling, no waiting rooms, no disruption to routines.
