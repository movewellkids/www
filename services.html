Conditions I Treat

Specialist physiotherapy for babies, children and young people across a wide range of musculoskeletal and developmental conditions — all delivered as home visits.

How I can help

I work with children from birth through to adolescence. Below are the conditions and presentations I see most often.

All sessions are delivered as home visits — I come to you. Currently covering East Dulwich, Dulwich Village, West Dulwich, West Norwood, Tulse Hill, Herne Hill and Gipsy Hill.

Infant & Baby

Torticollis & Plagiocephaly Baby MOT

Assessment and treatment for infants with a head turning preference, including congenital muscular torticollis (CMT) and associated plagiocephaly (flat head syndrome). Early treatment produces the best outcomes.

Book for this condition →

Developmental Delay Baby MOT

Support for babies and children who are late reaching physical milestones such as rolling, sitting, crawling or walking. I provide targeted, play-based programmes to build strength, coordination and confidence.

Book for this condition →

Positional Talipes

Early intervention and exercise programmes for infants with positional talipes (positional club foot), supporting correct foot positioning and development from the earliest weeks of life.

Book for this condition →

Children & Young People

Musculoskeletal Pain & Injury

Assessment and treatment of joint, muscle and soft tissue pain or injury in children and young people, including strains, sprains and growth-related conditions such as Sever's disease and Osgood-Schlatter.

Book for this condition →

Hypermobility & EDS

Assessment and management of joint hypermobility syndrome and hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome (hEDS) in children and adolescents, addressing pain, joint instability and fatigue through targeted exercise and education.

Book for this condition →

Gait Abnormalities

Specialist assessment of walking patterns including toe walking, intoeing (pigeon toes) and out-toeing, with targeted treatment and gait retraining programmes tailored to your child's needs.

Book for this condition →

Chronic Pain

A compassionate, evidence-based approach to helping children and young people manage persistent pain, with a focus on improving function, rebuilding confidence and enhancing quality of life.

Book for this condition →

Juvenile Arthritis

Physiotherapy support for children and adolescents with juvenile idiopathic arthritis (JIA), aimed at maintaining mobility, building strength and supporting participation in everyday activities and school life.

Book for this condition →

Sports Injury

Helping young athletes and active children recover safely from sports injuries and return to the activities they love, with structured rehabilitation and return-to-sport guidance.

Book for this condition →

Postural Concerns

Assessment and advice for children with postural issues, including screening for scoliosis and kyphosis, postural awareness programmes and ergonomic advice for school and home environments.

Book for this condition →

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Structured rehabilitation following orthopaedic or other surgery, supporting children to safely regain strength, range of movement and function through a carefully progressive programme.

What to expect

Starting something new can feel daunting. Here's what a first appointment looks like.

Get in touch

Email me with a brief outline of your child's needs. I'll respond within one working day to answer any questions and find a convenient time for your first home visit.

Home assessment

I'll visit your home for a comprehensive 60-minute assessment — observing your child's movement, discussing their history and working with you to agree on goals. Parents are encouraged to be part of the whole session.

Your personalised plan

Following the assessment, I'll create a tailored treatment plan and share it with you in writing. Every element will be explained clearly so you feel informed and confident about the path ahead.
