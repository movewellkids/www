Our Services

At MoveWell Kids, every child receives dedicated, one-to-one physiotherapy tailored entirely to them. Rebecca takes the time to get to know each child — building genuine rapport, listening carefully, and creating a space where children feel safe, understood, and valued. By truly understanding each child's individual needs, challenges, and goals, she is able to develop a personalised treatment plan that addresses the root of any difficulties. Central to her approach is helping children build confidence in their own bodies: celebrating small wins, working through challenges at the child's own pace, and empowering families with the knowledge and tools to support progress at home. Whether your child is working towards a developmental milestone, managing a physical condition, or recovering from an injury, Rebecca is committed to helping them move better, feel stronger, and thrive.

What We Offer

Paediatric Physiotherapy Assessment

Every journey begins with a thorough initial assessment. Using age-appropriate and play-based techniques, Rebecca takes the time to observe your child's movement, understand their history, and listen to your concerns as a family. This forms the foundation of a personalised treatment plan tailored to your child's specific needs and goals.

Developmental Delay & Gross Motor Skills

If your child is taking longer than expected to reach milestones such as rolling, sitting, crawling, or walking, early physiotherapy support can make a real difference. Rebecca works with children to build the strength, balance, and coordination needed to achieve key physical milestones, helping them grow in ability and confidence at their own pace.

Hypermobility

Children with hypermobility — sometimes called being "double-jointed" — may experience joint pain, fatigue, poor coordination, or frequent trips and falls. Rebecca helps children and their families understand hypermobility and its impact, developing targeted exercise programmes to improve joint stability and strength, and providing practical day-to-day management advice.

Gait & Walking

Toe walking, in-toeing, out-toeing, flat feet, and unusual walking patterns are among the most common concerns parents bring to a paediatric physiotherapist. Rebecca assesses your child's gait and lower limb alignment to determine whether a variation is within normal development or requires treatment, providing tailored intervention and clear, practical guidance.

Musculoskeletal Conditions & Joint Pain

From growing pains and juvenile arthritis to conditions such as Osgood-Schlatter's disease, Sever's disease, and Perthes disease, Rebecca assesses and treats a wide range of musculoskeletal conditions in children. Treatment focuses on reducing pain, restoring comfortable movement, and equipping children with the tools to stay active and healthy.

Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Active children and young athletes can be vulnerable to sprains, strains, stress fractures, and overuse injuries. Rebecca provides evidence-based assessment and rehabilitation specifically designed around the needs of the growing body, supporting a safe and confident return to sport and physical activity.

Postural Assessment & Support

Poor posture can lead to discomfort, pain, and difficulty with everyday activities. Rebecca assesses your child's posture, identifies any contributing factors, and develops a programme of targeted exercises and practical guidance to support better alignment and long-term physical wellbeing.

Torticollis (Wry Neck)

Torticollis causes the head to tilt or turn to one side, and is most commonly seen in young babies. Early physiotherapy is highly effective, and Rebecca's approach combines gentle stretching, strengthening exercises, and positioning advice to restore full neck movement and prevent long-term complications.
