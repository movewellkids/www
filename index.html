Welcome to MoveWell Kids Physiotherapy! We specialize in pediatric physiotherapy services to help children improve their mobility and overall well-being.

Rebecca qualified in Bsc Physiotherapy in 2008 at the University of Birmingham. She has extensive experience in musculoskeletal physiotherapy, specialising in paediatrics, including treating children with sports injuries, congenital conditions, hypermobility, chronic pain, and developmental delay. I have over 15 years experience treating children across various specialties including cardiorespiratory and neurology but my main interest now lies in treating those with musculoskeletal problems. 12 years NHS experience, 3 years private physiotherapy experience in Dubai,
