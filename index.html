How I can help your child

I work with babies, children and young people across a wide range of musculoskeletal and developmental conditions.

🦴

Musculoskeletal Pain & Injury

Assessment and treatment of joint, muscle and soft tissue pain or injury in children and young people.

👶

Baby MOT

Specialist assessment for infants — including torticollis, plagiocephaly and developmental delay. Early intervention brings the best results.

🤸

Hypermobility & EDS

Assessment and management of joint hypermobility and hEDS in children and adolescents, addressing pain, instability and fatigue.

🚶

Gait Abnormalities

Specialist assessment of walking patterns including toe walking, intoeing and out-toeing, with targeted treatment programmes.

⚽

Sports Injury

Helping young athletes recover safely from sports injuries and return to the activities they love.

🏥

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Structured rehabilitation following surgery, supporting children to safely regain strength, movement and function.

15 years of specialist experience, at your door

I work closely with parents throughout the whole process, making sure treatment plans and home exercise programmes are practical and genuinely manageable for busy families.
