# MoveWell Kids Physiotherapy

> Specialist paediatric physiotherapy for babies, children and teenagers in Dulwich and South London. All sessions are home visits. HCPC registered.

## About

Rebecca is a specialist paediatric physiotherapist with more than 15 years of clinical experience, including 12 years in the NHS. She holds a BSc Physiotherapy from the University of Birmingham and is HCPC registered (PH90202), MCSP and MAPCP qualified. She works exclusively with babies, children and teenagers, delivering sessions as home visits across South London.

## Services

- **Musculoskeletal Pain & Injury** — Assessment and treatment of joint, muscle and soft tissue pain (also: growing pains, benign limb pains of childhood, heel pain in children, knee pain in growing teens), including growth-related conditions such as calcaneal apophysitis (Sever's) and tibial tuberosity apophysitis (Osgood-Schlatter's)
- **Developmental Delay** — Support for children not meeting expected physical milestones (also: gross motor delay, global developmental delay (GDD), motor milestone delay)
- **Baby MOT** — Personalised developmental screening for babies (also: baby development check, infant development assessment, newborn physical screening), including musculoskeletal and neurological profile review
- **Torticollis & Plagiocephaly** — Assessment and treatment for babies with a head turning preference, torticollis (wry neck, twisted neck) or flat head (plagiocephaly, brachycephaly, flat head syndrome, preferential head position)
- **Hypermobility & EDS** — Physiotherapy for hypermobile children and teenagers (also: double-jointed, joint laxity, loose joints, bendy joints), including hypermobility spectrum disorder (HSD), hEDS, Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome
- **Gait Abnormalities** — Assessment of toe walking (idiopathic toe walking, equinus gait), in-toeing (pigeon toed, femoral anteversion, internal tibial torsion, metatarsus adductus), out-toeing (duck-footed, femoral retroversion, external tibial torsion) and other gait concerns
- **Sports Injuries** — Rehabilitation for young athletes returning to sport after injury (also: youth sports injury, return-to-sport rehab, paediatric sports physiotherapy)
- **Chronic Pain** — Management of persistent pain conditions in children and teenagers (also: paediatric pain, complex regional pain syndrome (CRPS), amplified musculoskeletal pain)
- **Juvenile Arthritis** — Physiotherapy support for children with juvenile idiopathic arthritis (JIA, juvenile rheumatoid arthritis) and other inflammatory conditions
- **Postural Assessment** — Evaluation and treatment of postural concerns including scoliosis (curved spine), kyphosis (hunched back, rounded upper back), bow legs (genu varum), and knock knees (genu valgum)
- **Positional Talipes** — Treatment for foot position concerns in newborns (also: positional clubfoot, foot turning in baby, curly foot)
- **Post-Surgical Rehabilitation** — Physiotherapy following orthopaedic or other surgery (also: post-operative physiotherapy, post-op rehab, orthopaedic rehab). Common procedures and conditions Rebecca rehabs in the community phase include:
  - **Fractures**: supracondylar humerus, distal radius / forearm, femur and tibia (post-ORIF, post-K-wire, post-flexible nail / TENS); hardware removal
  - **Knee**: ACL reconstruction, meniscal repair, MPFL / patellar stabilisation, osteochondritis dissecans (OCD) drilling
  - **Hip**: post-Pavlik harness DDH follow-up, post-spica DDH, Salter / Pemberton osteotomy, Perthes' disease (post-arthrogram or osteotomy), SCFE / SUFE pinning
  - **Foot & ankle**: Ponseti casting and post-Achilles tenotomy follow-up (clubfoot / talipes equinovarus), tarsal coalition resection, calcaneal lengthening flatfoot reconstruction
  - **Spine**: posterior spinal fusion (scoliosis), growing rods / MAGEC, kyphosis correction
  - **Upper limb**: brachial plexus microsurgery / nerve transfer (post-Erb's palsy surgical repair), shoulder stabilisation, hardware removal
  - **Guided growth / limb length**: 8-plate / epiphysiodesis, limb lengthening (PRECICE, Ilizarov frame) post-op community phase
- **Erb's Palsy** — Targeted exercise programmes to improve arm movement, strength and function (also: brachial plexus injury, neonatal brachial plexus palsy, birth-related arm weakness)
